# NNNN — Short title in the imperative

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context

What forced a decision here? The constraints, the problem, and anything a reader
three months from now won't remember. Keep it to a paragraph.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why it was rejected.
- **Option B** — why it was rejected.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what has to change if it's ever
reversed.
